◆ HOW WE KNOW

The Evidence Standard

Every claim in every file is verified against the primary record before it is spoken aloud. The method, on the record:

01 · PRIMARY DOCUMENTS FIRST

Patents, court transcripts, census ledgers, period newspapers. A file begins where the paper trail begins — not where the legend does.

CLEARED
02 · THE 100-POINT REVIEW

Before narration, every script clears a 100-point editorial review — sourcing, dates, dollar figures, names, and context.

03 · THE NAMED MECHANISM

Never a vague “they.” The statute, the bank, the board, the court — named, dated, and cited in the record.

04 · AMENDED ON THE RECORD

When a stronger source surfaces, the file is corrected and the correction is logged. The archive owes its readers the errata.
